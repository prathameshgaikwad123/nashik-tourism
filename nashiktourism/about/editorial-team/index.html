About this site

NashikTourism.com Editorial Team

How the guides on NashikTourism.com are researched, checked and updated.

Who writes the guides

The guides on NashikTourism.com are written and maintained by the NashikTourism.com Editorial Team. We are an independent travel and information guide to Nashik and Trimbakeshwar. We are not a government body, a temple trust, the Nashik–Trimbakeshwar Kumbh Mela Authority or an official tourism organisation, and we say so on every page.

How we research

  • Official sources first. Government bodies, the Authority, the railways, MSRTC, the police and an attraction’s own site rank above news, and news ranks above travel blogs. The full registry, by tier, is on our sources page.
  • A report is not a fact. A news report becomes a fact on this site only when an official source supports it. Until then it is labelled Reported.
  • We show disagreements. When sources give different figures (distances are a common case) we show all of them and label the row Sources differ rather than picking one.

How we verify

Every claim that matters carries its own status: Verified (an editor read the cited source and it supports the claim, with the date), Reported, Sources differ or Not yet verified. Anything that could hurt a traveller if it were wrong needs a named human approver before it is published. The rules are on the editorial policy page and are enforced by the site’s build, not just stated.

How we keep guides current

Each guide shows a Last updated date and a Last verified date. Time-sensitive notices expire and move to the archive. Changes are logged on each page’s change history.

Sponsored content

Sponsored content is always labelled, links in it use rel="sponsored nofollow", sponsors have no control over facts, and a sponsored page is never marked Verified. See the sponsored-content policy.

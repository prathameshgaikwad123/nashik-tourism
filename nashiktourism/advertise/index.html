Who reads the site

NashikTourism.com is an independent, source-checked guide. Its readers are mainly:

  • Pilgrims visiting Trimbakeshwar, Ramkund and the other temples and ghats.
  • Kumbh Mela 2027 visitors planning transport, stays, dates and passes.
  • Weekend travellers from Mumbai and Pune looking at vineyards, hill country and day trips.

Audience statistics will be published here once they are confirmed from our analytics and Search Console. We do not quote traffic numbers we have not measured. Ask us for current figures when you enquire.

What you can sponsor

Sponsored guide

Featured listing

A labelled listing for a hotel, tour, restaurant or vineyard on a relevant page. Listings are never mixed into editorial rankings or "best of" picks.

Newsletter sponsorship

A labelled sponsor slot in the Kumbh 2027 updates channel, once that channel has subscribers to report.

Kumbh-season banner

A labelled banner placement on selected Kumbh pages during the peak period.

Editorial independence and labelling

  • Sponsored content is always labelled with a visible Sponsored tag and a disclosure line on the page.
  • Outbound links in sponsored content use rel="sponsored nofollow".
  • Sponsors never control the facts. They cannot buy editorial rankings, recommendations or a change to a figure. Guides, distances and dates stay under the editorial policy, with the same sourcing rules, and sponsors get no preview or veto of editorial pages that mention them.
  • A sponsored page is never marked Verified. The “Verified” status is reserved for claims an editor has checked against an official source.
  • We will decline anything that could mislead a traveller: unverified claims, unsafe advice, or a fake “pass” or booking for the Mela.
  • Affiliate links to booking sites are marked as sponsored; see the disclosure.

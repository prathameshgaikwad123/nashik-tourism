Best Time to Visit Nashik
Month by Month Guide

Nashik offers something different every season — the Kumbh Mela, wine-estate events, monsoon scenery, harvest. Here is a general guide to when to visit, based on what you want.

Travel TipsApril 20268 min read

How current is this guide?

Seasons, prices and arrangements change. Details in this guide have not been verified against official sources. Check before you travel. See our sources.

Nashik can be worth a visit in every season. The answer depends entirely on what you're looking for. This guide breaks it down by month and by travel type, in general terms; weather and festival dates change from year to year.

📅 Quick Answer

Kumbh Mela: the Simhastha is reported to run from 31 October 2026 to 24 July 2028, with Amrit Snan dates reported for August and September 2027 (reported, not yet confirmed by the Authority; see our dates section) • Wine & vineyards: the cooler months • Comfortable sightseeing: the cooler months, roughly October to March • Monsoon scenery: July–September • Budget travel: the hot months, April–June (prices not verified)

Month by Month — Nashik Weather and What's On

January ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Weather: Cool season, generally one of the most comfortable times of year. What's on: Wine-estate harvest activity and events may take place in the cooler months; dates vary by year and organiser, so check with the venue. Ideal for combining pilgrimage with vineyard visits.

February ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Weather: Cool to warm, usually sunny and dry. What's on: Wine-estate events and harvest activity may still be on; check with the venue. See the festivals note below for Hindu festivals such as Mahashivratri at Trimbakeshwar.

March ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Weather: Getting warmer, though mornings are usually still pleasant. What's on: Cool-season wine-estate activity may be winding down; check with the venue.

April ⭐⭐⭐

Weather: Hot; summer is arriving. What's on: Fewer visitors may be expected in the hot months and some travellers find it easier on the budget, but this site has not verified seasonal prices or crowds.

May & June ⭐⭐

Weather: Hot and dry, with humidity building before the monsoon. What's on: Usually a quieter tourist period. Suits travellers who prefer fewer crowds at temples and can cope with the heat; prices have not been verified.

July & August ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Kumbh 2027)

Weather: Monsoon; expect rain, which can be heavier in the hills than in the city. What's on in 2027: The first and second Amrit Snan are reported for 2 August and 31 August 2027 (not yet confirmed by the Authority). Attendance estimates vary widely and none is confirmed; plan for rain and crowds. Bhandardara and Igatpuri are popular for monsoon scenery, but check conditions and local advice before heading into the hills in heavy rain.

September ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Kumbh 2027)

Weather: Late monsoon, easing. What's on in 2027: The third Amrit Snan is reported for 11 September (Nashik) and 12 September (Trimbakeshwar) 2027 (not yet confirmed by the Authority).

October ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Weather: Post-monsoon, when the landscape tends to be green. Generally one of the better months to visit. What's on: The Simhastha Kumbh Mela is reported to open with a flag-hoisting on 31 October 2026 (reported, not yet confirmed by the Authority). Often overlooked but arguably one of Nashik's most beautiful months.

November ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Weather: Clear and cool; generally a comfortable time to visit. What's on: Vineyards tend to look lush after the monsoon. The tourist season is generally getting under way, so book ahead if you can; this site has not verified availability or prices.

December ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Weather: Cool, with chilly evenings and nights. Generally pleasant conditions. What's on: Usually a popular time to travel, so expect more demand for hotels; prices have not been verified. Year-end events at vineyards may be held; check with the venue.

Festivals: Hindu festivals such as Mahashivratri, Holi, Ganesh Chaturthi, Navratri, Dussehra, Diwali and Kartik Purnima follow the lunar calendar, so their dates change every year. This guide does not give festival dates; check a current calendar and, for the Kumbh Mela, our dates section.

Summary by Travel Type

  • First-time tourist: The cooler months — the most comfortable weather, plus wine-estate activity
  • Kumbh Mela pilgrim 2027: Amrit Snan dates reported for 2 August, 31 August, 11 September (Nashik) and 12 September (Trimbakeshwar) 2027; not yet confirmed by the Authority
  • Wine lover: The cooler months — vineyards at their best, plus any events the estates run
  • Nature and trekking: The post-monsoon months, or the monsoon for waterfalls around Bhandardara (check conditions and safety before you go)
  • Budget travel: April–June — heat, with fewer visitors likely (prices not verified)
  • Festival experience: Check the current year's festival calendar, as Hindu festival dates change every year

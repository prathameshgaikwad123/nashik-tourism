How to Reach Nashik for Kumbh Mela 2027 — Train, Bus & Flight Guide

Attendance estimates for Nashik Kumbh Mela 2027 vary widely and none is confirmed, but planning your journey early is sensible. Whether you're coming from Mumbai, Pune, Delhi or abroad, this guide covers the main ways of reaching Nashik and what to check before you travel. Journey figures here are approximate and not verified; confirm them with the operators (see our sources and the transport hub).

🚌

By Bus

MSRTC and private operators serve Nashik. Check routes, timings and fares with the operator.

By Flight

Ozar Airport is the airport serving Nashik. Scheduled flights are unverified; check with the airlines.

By Road (Drive)

About 2.5–3 hrs from Mumbai in normal traffic (approximate; not verified). Expect it to be slower on busy days.

Reaching Nashik by Train

Train is a common way to reach Nashik, especially from Mumbai and Pune. The railway station most visitors use is Nashik Road Railway Station; from there you will need a local transfer to Ramkund ghat (distance and time not verified, so check current options).

Trains from Mumbai and Pune to Nashik

Several express trains connect Mumbai and Pune with Nashik Road. We do not list train names or numbers here because they and their timings change. Check names, numbers, running days and timings on the railways’ own systems (see our sources). A fast train from Mumbai takes about 2.5–3 hours (approximate; not verified).

⚡ Booking Tip

Trains around the reported Amrit Snan dates (2 Aug, 31 Aug and 11–12 Sep 2027) may be in heavy demand, and seats may sell out. Booking windows are set by the railways, so check them on the railways’ own systems. The Railways say special trains are planned; no numbers, routes or dates have been published that we could locate. Check live updates and the railways’ own systems regularly.

From Nashik Road Station to Ramkund / Trimbakeshwar

  • To Ramkund (Panchavati): local autos, cabs and buses are the usual options; fares and journey times vary and are not verified here
  • To Trimbakeshwar: shared or private vehicles and buses are the usual options; fares and journey times vary and are not verified here (see the transport hub)
  • No shuttle-bus plan from the railway station to the ghats has been found in any source we could locate

Reaching Nashik by Bus

Buses are another common option, especially for travellers from within Maharashtra.

MSRTC (State Transport) Buses

MSRTC, the state transport corporation, runs buses to Nashik from many Maharashtra cities. Routes, frequency, fares and boarding points vary, so check them with MSRTC before you travel (see our sources).

Private Buses

Private bus operators also serve Nashik from other cities and states. Check the operator, route and booking terms yourself, and book early for busy dates.

📍 Nashik Bus Terminals

The main stand is usually given as the Nashik CBS (Central Bus Stand), with the Panchavati bus stand nearer Ramkund ghat (not verified; confirm with MSRTC or your operator).

Reaching Nashik by Flight

Ozar Airport serves Nashik and is about 25 km from the city (approximate; not verified). A new integrated terminal has been reported as administratively approved; an approval is not a completion date.

Flights to Nashik (Ozar)

Whether scheduled commercial flights operate from Nashik (Ozar), on which routes and how often, is unverified here and set by the airlines and the airport operator. Check the airlines and the Airports Authority of India before planning around a flight to Nashik, and keep an alternative route in mind.

⚠️ For International Travellers

If flying from abroad, you can arrive through a major international airport such as Mumbai's Chhatrapati Shivaji International Airport and continue to Nashik by road or rail (see the sections above and the transport hub). Pre-book your Nashik hotel before landing.

Reaching Nashik by Road (Self Drive)

If you're driving from Mumbai, the drive is about 167 km and takes about 2.5–3 hours in normal traffic (approximate; not verified). Use a current maps app for the route, and expect delays on busy days.

Key Road Routes

  • From Mumbai: about 167 km, about 2.5–3 hrs in normal traffic
  • From Pune: about 210 km, about 3.5–4 hrs in normal traffic
  • From other cities: check a current maps app for the route and time; we have no verified figures to give
  • All figures are approximate and not verified; highway numbers and road conditions change, so we do not list them
⚠️ Kumbh Traffic Warning

Roads into Nashik may be very congested on the reported Amrit Snan dates. Traffic and parking arrangements have not been announced in any source we could locate; they are expected to be set by the police and the Authority and may change from day to day. Consider arriving a day or more before, follow police instructions and check local news before travelling.

Top Travel Tips for Kumbh Mela 2027

01

Book Everything Early

Train and bus seats and hotel rooms may be hard to get around the Amrit Snan dates. Booking early is sensible.

02

Arrive a Day Before

Consider arriving the day before Amrit Snan. Roads into Nashik may be very congested on the day itself.

03

Use Off-Peak Dates

Other bathing dates fall during the Mela besides the reported Amrit Snan dates. A date away from them may be quieter, though we cannot say how much. See dates and sources.

04

Download Offline Maps

Download Nashik city maps on Google Maps offline. Mobile data may be slow in large crowds.

05

Nashik vs Trimbakeshwar

Decide which site you want first. The third Amrit Snan is reported as the Vaishnava akharas’ snan at Nashik (Ramkund) and the Shaiva akharas’ snan at Trimbakeshwar (reported, not confirmed).

06

Light Packing Only

You'll be in large crowds. Leave valuables at your hotel. Carry a small waterproof bag for the ghat.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is the nearest airport to Nashik?

Ozar Airport serves Nashik and is about 25 km from the city (approximate; not verified). Whether scheduled flights operate from it is unverified, so check the airlines. You can also fly into Mumbai and continue to Nashik by road or rail.

How far is Nashik from Mumbai by train?

Nashik Road Railway Station is approximately 2.5–3 hours from Mumbai by fast train (approximate; not verified). Several express trains connect Mumbai with Nashik Road; check names, numbers and timings on the railways’ own systems.

Will there be special trains for Kumbh Mela 2027?

The Railways say special trains are planned, including long-distance specials and short-distance MEMU services. No numbers, routes or dates have been published that we could locate. Check the railways’ own systems and live updates.

What is the best time to arrive in Nashik for Amrit Snan?

Consider arriving the day before your chosen Amrit Snan date. Procession timings and road closures have not been announced in any source we could locate, so check the latest notices for both before you travel.

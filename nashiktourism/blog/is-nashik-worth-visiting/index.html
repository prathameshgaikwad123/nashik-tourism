15 Best Places to Visit in Nashik
2026 Guide + Hidden Gems

Our editors' selection of 15 places in Nashik, covering temples, vineyards, caves and quieter spots — to help you plan your trip.

Nashik GuidesMay 202614 min read

How current is this guide?

Seasons, prices and arrangements change. Details in this guide have not been verified against official sources. Check before you travel. See our sources.

Here are 15 places in Nashik, selected by our editors as a starting point for your trip — from the famous Jyotirlinga to quieter sunset spots. Opening hours, entry fees, access and conditions have not been verified for any of them, so check locally before you go.

🛕 1. Trimbakeshwar Temple

One of the 12 Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva, widely regarded as a spiritual centre of the Nashik region. The three-faced Shiva linga and the temple’s connection to the Kumbh Mela make it a major draw.

Tip: Early mornings are often calmer, but temple timings and queues vary and have not been verified; check with the temple.

🌊 2. Ramkund

The sacred bathing ghat on the Godavari River, important to the Kumbh Mela. Even outside festival time, the evening aarti here can be deeply moving (times vary; check locally).

🍷 3. Sula Vineyards

A big part of why Nashik is often called a wine capital of India. Wine tasting, scenic vineyard views and a relaxed vibe that suits couples and friends. If you plan to taste wine, check the minimum legal drinking age and carry ID.

🏞️ 4. Pandavleni Caves

Ancient Buddhist caves carved into rock, offering a short climb and city views.

🌿 5. Anjneri Hill

Believed birthplace of Lord Hanuman. The trek is often described as manageable and rewards you with greenery and a spiritual-adventure mix; conditions vary with the weather, so take care, especially in the monsoon.

🌄 6. Brahmagiri Hills

Traditionally regarded as the source of the Godavari River. A scenic trek with mythological importance, popular in the monsoon; trails can be slippery and weather can change quickly, so take care and check local conditions.

🛕 7. Kalaram Temple

Famous for its black stone idol of Lord Rama. Worth a visit for its architecture and tranquil atmosphere.

🌳 8. Sita Gufa

A small cave linked to the Ramayana, where Sita is said to have stayed. Located in Panchavati, close to Ramkund.

🌊 9. Dudhsagar Falls Nashik

Seen at their fullest in the monsoon, these falls are a nature escape from the city. Carry a raincoat. Take care near waterfalls and slippery rocks, and check local conditions and access first.

🛕 10. Someshwar Temple

A Shiva temple beside a river, away from the main tourist circuit; it may suit quiet reflection.

🌄 11. Harihar Fort

Known for its steep, near-vertical stairs. A challenging trek with big views from the top. The stairs can be dangerous, especially when wet, so check current conditions and any access restrictions before you go.

🛍️ 12. Saraf Bazaar

A traditional market area for local shopping — brassware, spices and jewellery are commonly mentioned. Evenings are often livelier; check market hours locally.

🍇 13. York Winery

Another winery in the area, and an alternative to Sula. Visitor facilities, tasting arrangements and opening hours have not been verified; check with the winery. If you plan to taste wine, check the minimum legal drinking age and carry ID.

🌅 14. Gangapur Dam

A reservoir that may suit sunsets and relaxed evenings. Check access and any restrictions before you go, and take care near the water.

🌄 15. Vaitarna Lake

A lake near Nashik that may suit a quiet day trip, set among hills. Its exact location, access and conditions have not been verified, so check before you go.

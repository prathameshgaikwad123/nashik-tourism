Kumbh Mela 2027
Snan Dates — Reported Calendar

The Amrit Snan dates reported so far for Nashik and Trimbakeshwar, with other traditional calendar occasions listed for reference.

How current is this guide?

This guide was written before the Kumbh Mela’s official arrangements were published. Dates, figures, rules, fares, timings and facilities it describes have not been verified against official sources, and some may be out of date or wrong. Where it differs from the dates below, the dates below — which carry their sources — take precedence. See live updates and the official sources.

Dates, from our fact database: Mela opens 31 Oct 2026 · Amrit Snan 2 Aug 2027, 31 Aug 2027, 11 Sep 2027 (Nashik) and 12 Sep 2027 (Trimbakeshwar) · Mela concludes 24 Jul 2028. ◐Reported, not yet confirmed Dates and sources

One common misconception about Kumbh Mela 2027 is that there are only a few important bathing dates. The Simhastha Kumbh Mela is reported to run from 31 October 2026 to 24 July 2028, and devotees traditionally bathe on further auspicious days between the Amrit Snan dates. The full list of bathing days has not been confirmed.

💡 Key Insight

You do not have to come on Amrit Snan days. Many devotees regard bathing on any auspicious day (a Parva Snan) as holy. A visit on a non-peak date may mean smaller crowds and easier travel and accommodation, but no crowd, access or cost arrangements have been announced for any date.

Which Date Should You Choose?

For Maximum Spiritual Significance — Amrit Snan Days

August 2, August 31, and September 11–12, 2027 (all reported, not yet confirmed). These are the days on which the akharas traditionally bathe in procession and the atmosphere is at its most intense. Expect very large crowds; if you attend, plan to arrive well ahead of time and check the Authority’s notices for any timing or access rules, which have not been announced in sources we could locate.

For a Peaceful Experience — Parva Snan Days

Other auspicious days between the Amrit Snan dates may see smaller crowds than the Amrit Snan days, but there is no crowd forecast and none of these days has been announced as a Mela bathing date. Purnima and Amavasya days are traditionally regarded as auspicious.

For Pilgrims Who Cannot Travel in Peak Months

The Mela is reported to conclude on 24 July 2028. By tradition, Kartik month (October–November 2027) is regarded as especially sacred for bathing in any tirtha. Whether any organised bathing events are planned outside the reported Amrit Snan dates has not been announced in sources we could locate, so check the latest notices before planning a visit.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many bathing dates are there in Nashik Kumbh Mela 2027?

The full list of bathing dates has not been confirmed. Amrit Snan dates have been reported for 2 August, 31 August, 11 September (Nashik) and 12 September (Trimbakeshwar) 2027. Devotees traditionally bathe on further auspicious days between them, but we have not found a published list of those days.

Can I visit Kumbh Mela 2027 after September 2027?

The Mela is reported to conclude on 24 July 2028. Whether there will be organised bathing events after the reported Amrit Snan dates has not been announced in sources we could locate. By tradition, Kartik month (October–November) is regarded as especially sacred for bathing; check the latest notices before planning.

What is the difference between Amrit Snan and Parva Snan?

Amrit Snan (also known as Shahi Snan) is the term for the major bathing days on which the akharas traditionally bathe in procession. Parva Snan generally refers to bathing on days of Hindu calendar significance such as Purnima, Amavasya, Ekadashi and Sankranti. Many devotees regard both as holy. Only the Amrit Snan dates have been reported for this Mela; we have not found an announced list of Parva Snan dates.

Nashik Kumbh Mela 2027
Parking & Shuttle Buses — What Is Reported

Can you drive to Ramkund or the Trimbakeshwar temple on an Amrut Snan day? What the Kumbh Mela Authority’s reported parking and bus plan says, and what is still not announced.

Kumbh Mela6 min read

By the NashikTourism.com Editorial Team · Last updated 25 Nov 2026 · Published 25 Nov 2026 · Last verified: Not yet verified against official sources

Quick answer

On Amrut Snan days you reportedly cannot drive to Ramkund or the Trimbakeshwar temple. The Kumbh Mela Authority's reported plan keeps private vehicles at outer parking areas, with about 4,500 MSRTC buses carrying pilgrims onward. Parking locations and fares have not been published; check official notices before you travel.

Official dates, verified against the Authority’s FAQ on 22 Nov 2026: Mela opens 31 Oct 2026 · Amrut Snan 2 Aug 2027, 31 Aug 2027, 11 Sep 2027 (Nashik) and 12 Sep 2027 (Trimbakeshwar) · Mela concludes 24 Jul 2028. ✓Verified 22 Nov 2026 Dates and sources

If you are driving to Nashik Kumbh Mela 2027, the most useful thing to know is that you should not expect to drive anywhere near the ghats on an Amrut Snan day. The Kumbh Mela Authority (NTKMA) has drawn up a parking and shuttle-bus plan built around that idea. This guide sets out what has been reported about it, what it means for your trip, and what has not been announced yet.

📅 Status as of 25 November 2026

Reported, not final. Everything below comes from press reports of the Authority’s planning in June 2026, which describe the figures as planning estimates that may be revised. We could not locate a published official parking map, list of parking sites, or fares. Check the Authority’s official website before you travel.

Can you drive to Ramkund during Kumbh Mela 2027?

On Amrut Snan days, reportedly not. According to the plan reported in June 2026, private vehicles will be kept at parking areas on the outskirts, and pilgrims will be carried the rest of the way by dedicated shuttle buses. The areas around Ramkund in Nashik and the Trimbakeshwar temple are planned to work mainly as pedestrian zones on those days (Punekar News, 2 June 2026; Punekar News, 10 June 2026).

The Amrut Snan dates, as published on the Authority’s official FAQ, are 2 August 2027, 31 August 2027, and 11 September 2027 (Nashik) and 12 September 2027 (Trimbakeshwar). See all Snan dates.

What is the three-tier parking plan?

The Free Press Journal describes the Authority’s transport plan in three tiers: outer parking zones, inner drop-off points, and pedestrian routes within the Ramkund and Trimbakeshwar temple precincts (Free Press Journal). Punekar News gives more detail on the parking side (10 June 2026):

  • Outer parking: where private vehicles arriving at the city’s entry points are stopped.
  • Outer 1: backup parking for when outer parking fills up.
  • Inner parking: reachable only by the MSRTC shuttle buses; the last transfer point before the walking-only ghat areas.
  • On Amrut Snan days, only the outer and outer 1 parking areas are reported to operate, leaving the inner city effectively free of private vehicles.

The same report puts the planned parking at 46 locations in Nashik and 26 in Trimbakeshwar, with separate areas for two-wheelers, cars and buses. The locations themselves have not been named in the reports we read.

How do the Kumbh shuttle buses work?

The Authority’s plan is for about 4,500 MSRTC buses in total, according to both the Free Press Journal and Punekar News. For pilgrims arriving by road, the reported split is:

RouteBuses (reported)
Nashik outer parking → inner areas near the ghats900
Nashik parking areas → Trimbakeshwar1,485
Internal circuit within Trimbakeshwar325

The buses are reported to be GPS-tracked and linked to the Authority’s command and control centre. No fares have been published in the sources we found, so we cannot say whether the shuttles will be free or paid.

Arriving by train: which stations will have shuttles?

Around 1,500 more buses are planned for rail passengers, according to Punekar News: about 520 from the railway stations to inner parking points and about 680 running directly to Trimbakeshwar. Five stations are named as part of the plan: Nashik Road, Deolali, Odha, Kherwadi and Kasbe Sukne. Special trains are proposed for peak periods, but no train numbers or timetables have been published that we could locate. See how to reach Nashik for the Kumbh.

Parking at Trimbakeshwar

Trimbakeshwar is a small town on a hillside road, so parking there is tighter than in Nashik. The plan reports 26 parking locations for Trimbakeshwar and a 325-bus internal circuit, with the temple area mainly pedestrian on Amrut Snan days.

The arrangement is not new. On busy Shravan Mondays, the police have already directed devotees from Nashik to park at Khambale and take shuttle buses to Trimbakeshwar (Lokmat Times). Expect something similar, on a much larger scale, during the Kumbh. For the road itself, see our Nashik to Trimbakeshwar distance and route guide.

What has not been announced yet

  • The names and map of the parking locations
  • Whether parking or the shuttle buses will be charged, and at what rate
  • Which vehicle rules apply on ordinary Mela days, as opposed to Amrut Snan days
  • Any vehicle pass or e-pass for private cars (see our e-pass guide)

Officials reviewed parking sites, capacity, access roads and entry and exit arrangements again in October 2026 (Free Press Journal), so the details are still being worked out. We will update this page when the Authority or the police publish them.

Tips if you are driving to the Kumbh

  • Plan to park and ride. Budget time for the shuttle and for walking the last stretch to the ghat.
  • Arrive the day before an Amrut Snan if you can, and stay somewhere you can reach without your car. See where to stay for the Kumbh.
  • Note where you parked (the parking area’s name or number) before you board a shuttle; the parking areas will be large and busy.
  • Carry water, a phone power bank and very little luggage. You may walk and queue for a long time.
  • Check the latest notices from the Authority and the Nashik police on the day before you travel, as arrangements may change at short notice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I take my car to Ramkund during Nashik Kumbh Mela 2027?

Not on Amrut Snan days, according to the plan reported in June 2026. Private vehicles are to stop at outer parking areas, and MSRTC shuttle buses take pilgrims towards the ghats. Rules for ordinary Mela days have not been announced.

How many parking areas will there be for the Kumbh?

Press reports of the Authority’s plan mention 46 parking locations in Nashik and 26 in Trimbakeshwar. These are planning figures, and the locations have not been published.

Will the Kumbh shuttle buses be free?

No fares have been announced in the sources we found. About 4,500 MSRTC buses are planned, including shuttles from the parking areas and from five railway stations.

Which railway stations will have shuttle buses to the Kumbh?

Nashik Road, Deolali, Odha, Kherwadi and Kasbe Sukne are named in the reported plan, with buses to inner parking points and directly to Trimbakeshwar.

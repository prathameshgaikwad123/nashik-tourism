Nashik Kumbh Mela 2027
Budget Guide — How Much Do You Need?

What to budget for on a budget, mid-range or comfortable trip, and how to estimate each cost from current quotes.

Planning8 min read

How current is this guide?

Hotel names, rates, availability and booking advice here have not been verified against each business’s own information. Prices change, especially around the Kumbh. Check directly with the hotel or the booking site, and see our sources.

Dates, from our fact database: Mela opens 31 Oct 2026 · Amrit Snan 2 Aug 2027, 31 Aug 2027, 11 Sep 2027 (Nashik) and 12 Sep 2027 (Trimbakeshwar) · Mela concludes 24 Jul 2028. ◐Reported, not yet confirmed Dates and sources

A common question from first-time Kumbh Mela planners: how much money should I bring? The honest answer is that it depends on your travel style and where you start from, and we have no verified prices to give you. This guide sets out what to budget for and how to estimate each cost from current quotes. Prices change, especially around the Kumbh.

⚠️ Important: Prices May Rise Around Amrit Snan

Hotels, private transport and some food vendors may charge more than usual on and around the reported Amrit Snan dates (2 Aug, 31 Aug and 11–12 Sep 2027). We cannot say by how much, so check current prices before you book and keep a buffer.

Money Tips for Kumbh Mela

  • Carry some cash — ATMs near the ghats may have long queues on busy days. Withdraw before arriving.
  • Carry both cash and UPI — some vendors may accept UPI apps, but mobile data may be slow in large crowds.
  • Book transport early — seats may be in heavy demand around the Amrit Snan dates. Fares and booking windows are set by the operators, so check them on the operators’ own systems.
  • Agree fares in advance — auto and taxi fares may be higher than usual in crowded periods. Agree the fare before getting in.
  • Do not count on free food — community kitchens may be run by religious organisations, but we have found no announcement of any, so carry money for meals.
  • Consider a less busy date — prices may be lower on dates away from the reported Amrit Snan dates, though we cannot say by how much.

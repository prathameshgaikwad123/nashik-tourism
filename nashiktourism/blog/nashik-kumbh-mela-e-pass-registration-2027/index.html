Nashik Kumbh Mela 2027
E-Pass & Registration — What Has Been Announced

Is registration mandatory? Is there an e-pass? What has been reported so far, what has not been announced, and how to avoid paying for a fake “pass”.

Kumbh Mela8 min read

No pass or registration scheme has been announced

We could not locate any official announcement of an e-pass or registration requirement for general visitors. Registration has been reported only for religious organisations and student volunteers. Parts of this guide describe a scheme in expectation, not as announced fact. Be cautious of anyone selling a “pass” before the Authority announces one, and see passes and registration on the Kumbh hub.

Dates, from our fact database: Mela opens 31 Oct 2026 · Amrit Snan 2 Aug 2027, 31 Aug 2027, 11 Sep 2027 (Nashik) and 12 Sep 2027 (Trimbakeshwar) · Mela concludes 24 Jul 2028. ◐Reported, not yet confirmed Dates and sources

One of the most searched questions as Nashik Kumbh Mela 2027 approaches is: "How do I get an e-pass for Kumbh Mela 2027?" and "Is registration mandatory?" This guide sets out what has and has not been announced, so you can avoid being misled by outdated content or by anyone selling a “pass”.

📅 Current Status

We could not locate any announcement of an e-pass or registration requirement for general visitors to Nashik Kumbh Mela 2027. What has been reported is narrower: online registration for religious and charitable organisations and for student volunteers, and administrative approval for a Simhastha web portal and mobile app. Whether that portal and app are live has not been confirmed. The dates quoted on this page are reported, not confirmed. See passes and registration on the Kumbh hub.

What Is the Kumbh Mela E-Pass?

An e-pass is, in general, a digital permit that organisers of large events may use to manage crowd flow, for example for vehicle entry into busy zones. No e-pass or registration scheme for general visitors to Nashik Kumbh Mela has been announced in any source we could locate, so this page cannot describe how one would work.

Whether any ticket, pass or charge will apply to attending the Mela or bathing at Ramkund or Kushavarta Kund has not been confirmed either way. Charges for particular services, such as parking, lodging or special access, are separate questions. Until the Authority says otherwise, treat anyone who tells you a pass is definitely required, or definitely not required, as unreliable, and check passes and registration on the Kumbh hub.

Do You Need to Register for Nashik Kumbh Mela 2027?

For general visitors, no registration requirement has been announced in sources we could locate. That is not the same as a confirmation that none will be introduced, so check for notices before you travel.

What has been reported so far:

  • Online registration (through a QR-based single window) for religious and charitable organisations
  • Registration for student volunteers (“Kumbhdoot”)
  • Administrative approval for a Simhastha web portal and Android/iOS mobile app, with launch not yet confirmed

None of these is a pass for ordinary visitors. Follow live updates and latest updates for changes.

Who Might Need an E-Pass?

The Authority has not published a list of who would need a pass, and we have not found one for any group of general visitors. In particular, we located no announced pass or registration scheme for:

  • Private vehicle owners — no vehicle pass, parking scheme or entry rule has been announced
  • Tour operators and pilgrim groups — no group registration scheme has been announced
  • Media and press, akhara and sadhu communities and Sadhugram camp organisers — we have not found published arrangements; ask the Authority directly

If you belong to one of these groups, or travel by any mode, do not assume either that a pass is needed or that it is not. Check the Authority’s own channels listed on our sources page shortly before you travel.

If a Pass or Portal Is Announced

A web portal and a mobile app for the Simhastha have been reported as approved, but we have not confirmed that either is live, and we cannot give you a web address or app name that we have verified. If a pass or registration is announced, how to apply will be set by the Authority.

  • Do not rely on an address or app link sent to you in a message, a social-media post or an advertisement.
  • Find the Authority’s own channels through our sources page and confirm there that a scheme exists before entering any personal details.
  • This site is an independent guide. It does not issue passes or take registrations.
⚠️ Beware of Fake E-Pass Websites

Large events can attract scam websites and messages that claim to sell passes, registrations or special access. Because no general-visitor pass has been announced, we advise you not to pay anyone for a Kumbh Mela “pass” or to enter identity or payment details on a site you reached through a forwarded message or advertisement. Whether any official scheme would carry a fee has not been announced. To check what has actually been announced, use the Authority’s own channels listed on our sources page.

Vehicle Entry Rules on Amrit Snan Days

No official parking, traffic or vehicle-restriction plan for the Mela has been located. Parking areas, one-way systems and vehicle restrictions are expected to be set by the police and the Authority and may change from day to day. What we can say from the sources we located:

  • No distance limit for private vehicles near Ramkund, and no parking-zone or check-post arrangement, has been announced.
  • The Railways say special trains are planned; no numbers, routes or dates have been published that we could locate.
  • No shuttle-bus plan, from Nashik Road station, the bus stands or anywhere else, has been found in any source we could locate.

The practical advice: do not assume you will be able to drive close to the ghats on Amrit Snan days. Plan on public transport or parking far away, and expect to walk some distance. Check parking and roads and the latest notices before you travel.

Step-by-Step: What to Do Now for Registration

  • General pilgrim: No registration or pass has been announced for general visitors, so there is nothing to apply for at present. Check for notices before you travel, and plan your travel and accommodation early.
  • Private vehicle owner: No vehicle pass or parking scheme has been announced. Watch for notices from the Authority and the police nearer the time.
  • Tour operator / group organiser: No group registration scheme has been announced. Contact the Authority directly, using the channels on our sources page.
  • International visitor: Visa and entry rules are set by the Government of India and are separate from anything the Mela may require; confirm them with the relevant Indian authorities. We have not found any Mela-specific registration for foreign visitors.
  • Everyone: Follow live updates for changes. This page will be revised if a pass or registration scheme is announced.

While You Wait — Book Your Hotel

No general-visitor pass has been announced. Rooms near the ghats may be limited on busy dates, so booking early is sensible.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Nashik Kumbh Mela 2027 registration mandatory?

No registration or pass requirement for general visitors has been announced in any source we could locate. Registration has been reported only for religious and charitable organisations and for student volunteers. We cannot confirm that none will be introduced, so check for notices before you travel.

Where do I find reliable information about Nashik Kumbh Mela 2027?

An authority to plan and coordinate the Simhastha has been reported as established by the Government of Maharashtra. Our sources page lists the Authority pages we located. A web portal and mobile app have been reported as approved; their launch has not been confirmed.

Will there be an e-pass for vehicles during Amrit Snan?

Nothing has been announced. No vehicle pass, parking plan or traffic plan has been located. Parking areas and vehicle restrictions are expected to be set by the police and the Authority and may change from day to day, so check the latest notices before you travel.

Can I get an e-pass from a third-party website?

No general-visitor e-pass has been announced, so be very wary of any website or message offering one. We advise you not to pay anyone for a Kumbh Mela pass. If a scheme is announced, confirm it through the Authority’s own channels on our sources page, including whether any fee applies.

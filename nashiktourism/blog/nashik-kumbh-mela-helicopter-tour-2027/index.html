Nashik Kumbh Mela 2027
Helicopter Service — What Has Been Reported

A helicopter service for pilgrims has been reported as under planning. Here is what is known, what has not been announced, and how to avoid being misled.

Kumbh Mela3 min read

A helicopter service is under planning, not announced

Press coverage reports government tourism planning for helicopter connections for pilgrims, including helipads. We could not locate an operator, routes, prices, seating details or a booking system. Prices, routes, portals and accessibility claims on this page are not confirmed — read it as speculation, not a booking guide. See live updates and the official sources.

Dates, from our fact database: Mela opens 31 Oct 2026 · Amrit Snan 2 Aug 2027, 31 Aug 2027, 11 Sep 2027 (Nashik) and 12 Sep 2027 (Trimbakeshwar) · Mela concludes 24 Jul 2028. ◐Reported, not yet confirmed Dates and sources

Visitors often ask whether there will be a helicopter service for Nashik Kumbh Mela 2027. The honest answer is that one has been reported as under planning, and nothing more has been confirmed. This page sets out what has been reported and what has not, so you can avoid planning around, or paying for, a service that may not exist.

📅 Current Status

Press coverage reports tourism-department planning for a helicopter service, including proposed helipads. We could not locate an operator, routes, prices, launch date or booking system. Treat anything beyond “under planning” as unconfirmed. See live updates for changes.

What Is the Nashik Kumbh Mela Helicopter Pilgrimage Circuit?

Press coverage reports tourism-department planning for a helicopter service on a Jyotirlinga circuit that includes Trimbakeshwar, along with proposed helipads. That is the extent of what has been reported.

We have not found a confirmed list of stops, an operator, a schedule, or any statement of whether the service would serve ordinary visitors to the Mela or how it would be booked. We therefore do not list routes or destinations here.

Why Visitors Are Asking About a Helicopter Service

The Mela is reported to be centred on two sites, Ramkund in Nashik and Kushavarta Tirtha in Trimbakeshwar, which are approximately 28 km apart by road (approximate; not verified). Roads may be busy on crowded days, so it is natural to wonder whether a helicopter could help. No such service has been announced, so please do not plan your trip around one. For ordinary ways of getting around, see our transport hub.

Routes and Timings: Not Announced

No route, flight time, departure frequency or schedule has been announced in any source we could locate, so we do not give any. If you see routes or timings quoted elsewhere for a Kumbh helicopter service, ask where they come from.

Costs: Not Announced

No prices have been announced. Any fare quoted for a Kumbh helicopter ride has no published source that we could locate. If you are asked to pay a deposit or full fare in advance, treat it with great caution.

Operator and Booking: Not Announced

No operator and no booking system has been announced. If a service is announced:

  • Confirm the operator and the booking channel through the Authority’s or the relevant department’s own channels, using our sources page, before paying anyone.
  • Do not pay anyone who contacts you offering helicopter seats before a service has been announced.
  • This site is an independent guide. It does not sell or take bookings for helicopter rides.

Should You Plan Around a Helicopter Service?

No. With no announced operator, route, price or booking system, we cannot tell you whether a helicopter would be worth it, or whether it will exist at all. Plan your Kumbh travel as though no helicopter service will be available, and treat one as a bonus if it is ever announced.

Whether a helicopter service could carry passengers with limited mobility, and under what conditions, has not been announced. For planning that does not depend on one, see Accessible Nashik and our transport hub.

Alternative Air View Options

We have not found an announcement of any other air-based sightseeing or transfer service for the Mela, and we do not list one. For getting around by road and rail, see our transport hub.

Book Your Nashik Accommodation Early

Whatever way you travel, rooms near Ramkund may be limited on busy dates, so booking early is sensible.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a helicopter service for Nashik Kumbh Mela 2027?

A helicopter service is reported as under planning. We could not locate an operator, routes, prices, a launch date or a booking system, so nothing about it is confirmed.

Can elderly pilgrims use the helicopter service?

This has not been announced. We have found no information on who could use a helicopter service, whether it could carry passengers with limited mobility, or what conditions would apply. See Accessible Nashik for planning that does not depend on one.

How do I book a helicopter for Nashik Kumbh Mela 2027?

No booking system has been announced, so there is nothing to book. Be wary of anyone offering helicopter seats now. If a service is announced, confirm it through the channels on our sources page before paying.

Is there a helicopter between Nashik and Trimbakeshwar?

No such route has been announced. By road the distance is approximately 28 km and the normal journey time approximately 45–60 minutes (approximate; not verified). Journeys can take longer on busy days, and we have no verified figure for that.

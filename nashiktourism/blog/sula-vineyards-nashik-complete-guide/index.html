Sula Vineyards Nashik
Complete Visit Guide

Wine tasting, vineyard walks, dining and a resort stay in the Sahyadri foothills near Nashik, plus the SulaFest wine and music festival when it is held.

Nashik GuidesApril 20268 min read

How current is this guide?

Opening hours, fees, facilities and arrangements in this guide have not been verified against the venue’s or authority’s own information, and can change on festival days. Check before you travel. See our sources.

Sula Vineyards is one of the best-known names in Nashik's wine tourism, a destination in its own right apart from the city's religious significance. Its estate lies in the Sahyadri foothills near Nashik.

📅 Quick Facts

Location: on the Gangapur Road side of Nashik (confirm the exact address with the estate) • Distance: about 15 km from Nashik city (not verified) • Open: hours vary; check with the estate before you go • Wine tasting: tastings from ₹300 have been quoted (not verified; check the estate)

What to Do at Sula Vineyards

Wine Tasting

Sula's tasting room is described as overlooking the vineyards, with tasting sessions on offer. Packages, prices and which wines are poured vary, so check with the estate; it may also be wise to ask about booking ahead on weekends. Check the minimum legal drinking age and carry ID if you plan to taste wine.

Vineyard Walk

Walks through the vineyards may be available; ask the estate whether guided walks run on the day you visit. The vines tend to look their best in the cooler months, and the harvest season may offer a chance to watch the picking, but timing varies from year to year.

Dining

The estate is understood to have restaurant and wine-bar options with vineyard views, where you can pair local wine with food. Which outlets are open, their menus and their prices change, so check with the estate.

Resort Stay

The estate is understood to offer a resort stay on site, with vineyard views. Room types, rates and availability have not been verified; check with the resort or the booking site you use.

SulaFest

SulaFest is the estate's wine and music festival. Whether it is held in a given year, and on which dates, is announced by the organisers; this site has not confirmed any dates, format or ticket prices. Check the estate's own announcements before you plan around it.

Sula Wines to Know Before You Visit

Ranges and labels change, so ask the estate what is currently on offer. Some names you may come across:

  • The Source Sauvignon Blanc — A white wine, crisp and tropical in style.
  • Dindori Reserve Shiraz — A red wine named after a Nashik-area region, full-bodied in style.
  • Rasa Cabernet Sauvignon — An earthy, structured red.
  • Brut — A sparkling wine.
  • Madera NV — A port-style dessert wine, usually enjoyed after a meal.

How to Reach Sula Vineyards from Nashik

  • Cab (app-based, where available, or local taxi): Usually the simplest option from the city. Fares and journey times vary.
  • Auto-rickshaw: Fares vary and are usually negotiated; agree before you set off.
  • Self drive: Head out of Nashik on Gangapur Road and use a maps app to find the estate. Check the route and road conditions before you travel.

Best Time to Visit Sula Vineyards

The cooler months are generally the most comfortable for an outdoor visit. The monsoon can make the setting lush and green, though rain may affect outdoor plans, so check what is open. If you're in Nashik for the Kumbh Mela, a vineyard visit is a contrast to the ghat experience. Weekday mornings are generally quieter than weekends.

Visiting Sula Alongside Kumbh Mela 2027

Nashik's identity is precisely this contrast — deeply spiritual and pleasurably worldly in the same city. A morning at Ramkund ghat followed by an afternoon at Sula Vineyards is a distinctive combination of experiences. See our 2-day Nashik itinerary for one way to structure it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to book in advance for Sula Vineyards?

Booking rules are set by the estate and can change. Check with the estate before you go, especially for weekends and festival days, to find out whether advance booking is needed.

Is Sula suitable for non-drinkers?

The setting, grounds and dining can be enjoyed without wine tasting. What is open to non-drinkers, and whether any age rules apply to children, is decided by the estate, so check before you go.

What is the entry fee?

Entry arrangements and tasting prices are set by the estate and have not been verified by this site. Check with the estate before you go.

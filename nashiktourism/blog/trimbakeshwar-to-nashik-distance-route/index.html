Trimbakeshwar to Nashik
Distance, Route & Travel Guide

How to travel between the two Kumbh Mela sites — by shared jeep, cab and bus, and what to check first.

Travel Guide5 min read

How current is this guide?

Routes, journey times, fares and train or bus names in this guide come from our earlier research and have not been verified. The Railways say special trains are planned, but no train numbers, routes or dates have been located, and shuttle-bus arrangements have not been announced in sources we found. Check the operators before you travel: see the transport hub. See live updates and the official sources.

Dates, from our fact database: Mela opens 31 Oct 2026 · Amrit Snan 2 Aug 2027, 31 Aug 2027, 11 Sep 2027 (Nashik) and 12 Sep 2027 (Trimbakeshwar) · Mela concludes 24 Jul 2028. ◐Reported, not yet confirmed Dates and sources

Nashik Kumbh Mela 2027 is reported to be held at two sites: Ramkund in Nashik city and Kushavarta Kund in Trimbakeshwar. Many pilgrims want to visit both. This guide covers how to travel between them and what to check during the Kumbh period. Distances and times are approximate and not verified; fares are not given because we have no verified figures.

Distance from Trimbakeshwar to Nashik

The distance from Trimbakeshwar to Nashik city centre is approximately 28 kilometres by road (approximate; not verified). Check a current maps app for the distance from where you are staying or arriving.

Shared Jeep

Shared jeeps are one way to travel between Nashik and Trimbakeshwar. Where they leave from, how often they run, how full they must be before leaving, and the fare all vary and have not been verified; ask locally or at your hotel.

Private Cab or Taxi

Private cabs offer door-to-door convenience. Ask your hotel or a local taxi operator, and agree the fare (one way or for a full day) before you travel. Whether app-based cabs serve this route is unverified here.

MSRTC State Bus

MSRTC (state transport) buses may serve this route. Check routes, timings, fares and the last return service with MSRTC (see our sources), and do not assume a late-evening return bus.

By Road (Self Drive)

Use a current maps app for the route. The normal journey time is approximately 45–60 minutes (approximate; not verified). It may take much longer on busy days. Traffic and parking arrangements for the Mela have not been announced in sources we could locate, so check notices before you set out, and do not assume you can park close to either site on busy days.

The Route — What You Pass On the Way

Places commonly mentioned along or near the Nashik to Trimbakeshwar route include:

  • Gangapur Dam, near Nashik
  • Anjaneri Hill, associated in tradition with Lord Hanuman
  • Brahmagiri Mountain, traditionally associated with the source of the Godavari
  • Trimbak town, at the end of the route, a pilgrim town

We have not verified distances to these places; check a current maps app.

During Kumbh Mela 2027 — What Changes

Arrangements for the Nashik–Trimbakeshwar route during the Mela have not been announced in any source we could locate. Things to keep in mind:

  • Traffic restrictions: No official parking, traffic or vehicle-restriction plan has been located. Any restrictions are expected to be set by the police and the Authority and may change from day to day, so check notices before you travel.
  • Shuttles and special services: No shuttle-bus plan, between Nashik and Trimbakeshwar or elsewhere, has been found in any source we could locate. The Railways say special trains are planned, but no numbers, routes or dates have been published.
  • Journey time: It may increase a lot on busy days. We have no verified figure, so allow generous extra time.
  • A possible strategy: Consider visiting Trimbakeshwar on a different day from Nashik (Ramkund) rather than trying to do both on a busy date.
💡 Pro Tip for Kumbh

The third Amrit Snan is reported as the Vaishnava akharas’ snan at Nashik (Ramkund) on 11 September 2027 and the Shaiva akharas’ snan at Trimbakeshwar (Kushavarta) on 12 September 2027. These dates are reported, not confirmed; check the latest notices, as dates and arrangements may change.

Which Should You Visit First — Nashik or Trimbakeshwar?

There is no single right order, and we have not found an official recommendation. Choose the order that suits your travel plans and your arrival point.

For example, if you are arriving by train at Nashik Road station, it may be simpler to visit Nashik first, then travel to Trimbakeshwar, and return to Nashik for your onward journey. Check temple and Mela notices for both sites before you go.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Trimbakeshwar from Nashik?

Trimbakeshwar is approximately 28 km from Nashik city centre by road (approximate; not verified).

How long does it take to go from Nashik to Trimbakeshwar?

Approximately 45–60 minutes by road in normal conditions (approximate; not verified). It may take longer on busy days; we have no verified figure for the Kumbh period.

What is the cheapest way to get from Nashik to Trimbakeshwar?

We have not verified fares for any option. Check MSRTC for bus fares and ask locally for shared vehicles, and agree any cab fare before you travel.

Is there a train between Nashik and Trimbakeshwar?

We have not found a direct rail service to Trimbakeshwar. The usual approach is to reach Nashik (for example Nashik Road station) and continue by road. Check the railways’ own systems to confirm.

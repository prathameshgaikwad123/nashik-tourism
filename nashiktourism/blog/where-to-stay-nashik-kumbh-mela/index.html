Nashik Kumbh Mela 2027 Accommodation: Where to Stay, Hotels & Tent Cities

Hotels & Stay 7 min read

By the NashikTourism.com Editorial Team · Last updated 25 Nov 2026 · Published 5 Apr 2026 · Last verified: Not yet verified against official sources

Quick answer

For Kumbh Mela 2027, look for stays near Ramkund and Panchavati in Nashik, in Trimbakeshwar, or elsewhere in the city with road access. Private tent cities approved by the Kumbh Mela Authority are reported for March to December 2027, but no approved list or prices have been published. Book early with a refundable option; rates are not verified.

Choosing the right area is the most important decision. Here are the four key zones:

Near Ramkund

Panchavati / Ramkund Area

Close to the Ramkund ghat, so often the most convenient area, and likely to be in high demand. Book early and check the distance on a maps app.

Near Kushavarta Kund

Trimbakeshwar

About 29–30 km from the Nashik CBS (Google Maps, 22 November 2026), near Kushavarta Kund and the Jyotirlinga temple. Convenient if you plan to attend events there.

Central

Nashik City Centre

May offer a wider choice of hotels than the ghat areas, though we have not verified availability. Check the distance to the ghats on a maps app and allow for local transport. An option if Panchavati is full.

Peaceful option

Gangapur / Deolali Area

Quieter outskirts. Suits those who want peace, but you would need to travel to the ghats on Snan days; check the distance and transport.

Dharamshalas & Ashrams

For pilgrims on a tight budget, dharamshalas (religious rest houses) and ashrams are one option. Terms, charges and availability vary and we have not verified any of them.

  • Dharamshalas and ashrams near the ghats — run by religious trusts and organisations; contact each directly about charges, rules and availability
  • Temples and religious organisations — some may have guest rooms for devotees; ask directly
  • Camps set up for the Mela — whether pilgrims can stay in akhara camps or in Sadhugram, and on what terms, has not been announced in sources we could locate. Private tent cities are a separate, reported plan; see tent cities below
💡 Dharamshala Booking Tip

Booking arrangements for dharamshalas vary; some may not take online bookings, so you may need to call or visit, and some may give preference to particular communities, so ask. We have not found any published list of Mela camps or approved tent cities yet. Check live updates and the Authority's own channels on our sources page for announcements.

Will there be tent cities at Nashik Kumbh Mela 2027?

Reported, not yet confirmed by an official notice we could open. On 29 September 2026, the Free Press Journal and Punekar News reported that the Nashik Trimbakeshwar Kumbh Mela Authority (NTKMA) has prepared a Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) that lets private landowners and operators set up temporary tent cities for pilgrims.

What those reports say about the plan:

  • Where: privately owned sites in Nashik district, covering Nashik and Trimbakeshwar, of at least two acres
  • When: tent cities may operate between March and December 2027
  • Who runs them: private landowners, or operators with a registered MoU with a landowner; a camp can open only after government departments approve its application
  • Minimum facilities required: toilets and bathing areas, drinking water, electricity, waste management, parking, food, 24x7 security and an emergency plan covering fire, crowds, medical help and evacuation, plus an electrical safety audit before guests move in

Neither report gives prices, a booking website, or a list of approved sites, and both say applications from operators were expected to open shortly. Until approved tent cities are announced, treat any tent city booking with care: ask the operator whether its site has NTKMA approval. Separately, the state tourism department has been reported to be planning its own tent city; no details or booking route had been published in sources we could locate.

Staying in Trimbakeshwar

If you're attending events at Kushavarta Kund (the third Amrut Snan is at Kushavarta Tirth on 12 September 2027, per the Authority’s official FAQ), staying in Trimbakeshwar itself may be easier than travelling from Nashik, about 29–30 km away from the CBS (Google Maps, 22 November 2026), on a very busy day.

  • Trimbakeshwar has some hotels and dharamshalas near the Jyotirlinga temple; we have not verified numbers, rates or availability
  • Availability may be limited around the Snan dates
  • Search a booking site with the location set to "Trimbakeshwar" for current options
  • You can stay in Nashik and travel to Trimbakeshwar on less busy days; the drive is roughly 55–60 minutes from the CBS in late-morning traffic (Google Maps, 22 November 2026)

Accommodation Tips for Kumbh Mela 2027

  • Book early for the Amrut Snan dates; we cannot say how far ahead rooms will fill
  • Non-refundable rates may be cheaper but are riskier; consider a refundable booking if your dates are uncertain
  • Ask about early check-in — you may arrive early on a Snan day
  • Consider arriving a day or two before an Amrut Snan and leaving afterwards, to avoid travelling on the busiest days
  • Verify the address on Google Maps before booking — a listing described as "Panchavati" may be further from Ramkund than you expect
  • Pack light — storage at budget properties may be limited
  • Ask about parking if driving — no official parking or traffic plan has been located, and restrictions near Ramkund may apply on busy days

Hotels, private transport and some food vendors may charge more than usual on and around the Amrut Snan dates (2 Aug, 31 Aug and 11–12 Sep 2027). Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book hotels for Kumbh Mela 2027?

We have no verified figure for how far ahead rooms will fill. For the Amrut Snan dates (2 August, 31 August and 11–12 September 2027), book as early as you can, ideally with free cancellation. For other dates during the Mela, booking early still helps; check availability and cancellation terms on the booking site.

Is it safe to stay in Nashik during Kumbh Mela?

We cannot give a safety assurance. Large gatherings carry risks such as crowding; see safety on the Kumbh hub for what has been reported. Standard travel precautions apply — keep valuables secure and stay aware of your surroundings in large crowds.

Are there any free accommodation options during Kumbh Mela?

Some dharamshalas and ashrams may offer low-cost or free stays, but terms and availability vary and we have not verified any. Whether free or low-cost accommodation will be provided for Mela visitors has not been announced in sources we could locate. Contact each place directly and watch live updates.

Will there be a tent city for pilgrims at Nashik Kumbh Mela 2027?

Reportedly, yes. In September 2026 news reports said the Kumbh Mela Authority (NTKMA) has an SOP allowing private operators to run approved tent cities in Nashik and Trimbakeshwar between March and December 2027. Prices, a booking website and the list of approved sites had not been published in sources we could locate, so check that any operator you book with has NTKMA approval.

Can I commute daily from Mumbai to Nashik during Kumbh?

It may be possible, but on busy days roads and trains may be crowded and delays are likely, and no special transport arrangements have been announced in sources we could locate. If you're attending an Amrut Snan, staying in Nashik the night before may be wise.

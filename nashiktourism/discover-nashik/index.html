Discover Nashik

Five very different places, all within reach of one city — a Jyotirlinga in the hills, an old riverside quarter, a vineyard estate, a hillside of rock-cut caves, and the Western Ghats.

Where to Go

Places to visit in Nashik

Each guide covers why the place is worth your time, how it fits into a trip, and what to read next.

Spiritual · Jyotirlinga

Trimbakeshwar Temple

Trimbakeshwar is a temple town in the hills west of Nashik, built around one of the twelve Jyotirlingas.

Open guide →
Heritage · Ghats

Panchavati & Ramkund

Panchavati is the old quarter of Nashik on the north bank of the Godavari, and Ramkund is the bathing ghat at its heart.

Open guide →
Wine · Leisure

Sula Vineyards

Sula is the best-known name in Indian wine and the estate that made Nashik a wine destination.

Open guide →
History · Heritage

Pandavleni Caves

Pandavleni is a group of caves cut into a hillside south of Nashik city — chambers, halls and carved facades worked directly into the rock.

Open guide →
Nature · Weekend

Igatpuri & Bhandardara

Igatpuri and Bhandardara sit in the Western Ghats near Nashik — hills, reservoirs and, through the monsoon, a great deal of water and green.

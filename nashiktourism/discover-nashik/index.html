Places to Visit in Nashik: Top Tourist Places List

Fourteen places to see in and around Nashik, with what each is, who it suits and how long to plan.

Quick answer

The top places to visit in Nashik are Trimbakeshwar Jyotirlinga, Ramkund and Panchavati with Kalaram Temple and Sita Gufa, Pandavleni Caves, Sula Vineyards and Someshwar Waterfall. Day trips include Igatpuri, Bhandardara and Saptashrungi. Opening hours and fees are not verified here; check locally.

By the NashikTourism.com Editorial Team · Last updated 22 Nov 2026 · Last verified: Not yet verified against official sources

Top places to visit in Nashik

Nashik's sights fall into four groups: pilgrimage (Trimbakeshwar, Ramkund and the Panchavati temples), heritage (Pandavleni Caves, the Coin Museum), wine (Sula Vineyards) and nature (waterfalls, dams and the Western Ghats). Each place below shows its own status: Verified means our editor checked it, Reported means the named sources say so, and Not yet verified means we have no supporting source yet. We show no opening hours or fees, because the sources disagree or we have not verified them.

  1. Trimbakeshwar Temple

    TempleBest for: PilgrimageTime: Half day ○Not yet verified

    A temple town in the hills west of Nashik, built around one of the twelve Jyotirlingas and the Kushavarta Kund. About 29–30 km from the Nashik CBS by road, roughly 55–60 minutes ✓Verified 22 Nov 2026 (Google Maps, 22 Nov 2026). See the distance and route guide.

    Sources: Google Maps driving route, measured 22 Nov 2026 (distance and time only)

  2. Ramkund and Panchavati

    Ghat and old quarterBest for: Pilgrimage, heritageTime: 1 to 2 hours ◐Reported, not confirmed

    The principal bathing ghat on the Godavari, in Nashik's Panchavati quarter. Tradition holds that Rama bathed here during his exile. The evening Godavari aarti is most often listed at about 7–8 PM; confirm locally (see the Ramkund guide).

    Sources: Nashik District tourism site: Panchvati; Outlook Traveller: Panchavati

  3. Kalaram Temple

    TempleBest for: PilgrimageTime: Under 1 hour ◐Reported, not confirmed

    An old temple in Panchavati known for its black idol of Rama, and one of Nashik's best-known temples. It is a short way from Ramkund; see the nearby-temples list in our Ramkund guide.

    Sources: Nashik District tourism site: Panchvati; Outlook Traveller: Panchavati

  4. Sita Gufa

    Cave shrineBest for: Pilgrimage, heritageTime: Under 1 hour ◐Reported, not confirmed

    A small cave in Panchavati where Sita is believed to have stayed during the exile. It is reported to be near Kalaram Temple, so the two are easily combined.

    Sources: Nashik District tourism site: Panchvati; Outlook Traveller: Panchavati

  5. Pandavleni Caves

    Rock-cut cavesBest for: HeritageTime: 1 to 1.5 hours ○Not yet verified

    A group of rock-cut chambers and halls on a hillside south of the city. Reaching them involves a climb (our earlier copy says around 200 steps; not verified), so check step-free access before you go.

    No source linked yet; see our guide or check locally.

  6. Sula Vineyards

    Vineyard and wineryBest for: Wine, leisureTime: Half day ○Not yet verified

    The estate most responsible for Nashik's wine reputation, set up for visitors with tasting, dining and a vineyard walk. Hours, tasting prices and booking rules are set by the estate; check with it.

    No source linked yet; see our guide or check locally.

  7. Someshwar Waterfall

    Waterfall and templeBest for: NatureTime: 1 to 2 hours ◐Reported, not confirmed

    A waterfall on the Godavari near Gangapur Road, close to the old Someshwar Shiva temple. Listings describe it as best in or just after the monsoon, with slippery rocks. Distances and timings differ between listings, so check locally.

    Sources: Trawell: Someshwar Waterfalls; Incredible India: Someshwar Temple

  8. Muktidham

    TempleBest for: PilgrimageTime: About 1 hour ○Not yet verified

    A white marble temple featuring replicas of India's twelve Jyotirlingas, a convenient way to see all twelve in one place. Our earlier copy gives no source for the details, so confirm timings and location before you go.

    No source linked yet; see our guide or check locally.

  9. Coin Museum

    MuseumBest for: HeritageTime: 1 hour ◐Reported, not confirmed

    A museum of coins at the Indian Institute for Research in Numismatic Studies campus on the Nashik–Trimbakeshwar road near Anjaneri. Sources differ on entry fee and list opening hours that include a closing day, so check with the museum before planning around it.

    Sources: Incredible India: Coin Museum; Trawell: Coin Museum

  10. Anjaneri Hill

    Hill and trekBest for: Nature, pilgrimageTime: Half day ◐Reported, not confirmed

    A hill fort on the Nashik–Trimbakeshwar road that tradition links to Hanuman's birth, with a hilltop temple and caves on the trek. It is a physical climb; check conditions and go in good weather.

    Sources: Incredible India: Anjaneri Hills; Wikipedia: Anjaneri

  11. Gangapur Dam

    Dam and lakeBest for: NatureTime: About 1 hour ○Not yet verified

    A dam on the Godavari near Nashik, mentioned as a landmark on the road towards Trimbakeshwar. We have no verified details on access or timings.

    No source linked yet; see our guide or check locally.

  12. Igatpuri

    Hill townBest for: Nature, weekend tripTime: Day trip ○Not yet verified

    A Western Ghats hill town in Nashik district, greenest in and just after the monsoon, and a common weekend trip from Mumbai and Pune. Roads and paths are slippery in the rain.

    No source linked yet; see our guide or check locally.

  13. Bhandardara

    Lake and waterfallsBest for: Nature, weekend tripTime: Day trip or overnight ◐Reported, not confirmed

    A lake and waterfall area around Arthur Lake in Akole taluka, Ahilyanagar district, just beyond the Nashik district line. Randha Falls and Umbrella Falls are the main sights, and fireflies are reported from about May to June.

    Sources: Maharashtra Tourism: Bhandardara Dam

  14. Saptashrungi

    Hill temple (day trip)Best for: PilgrimageTime: Full day ◐Reported, not confirmed

    A hill temple at Vani in Kalwan taluka, Nashik district, counted among the three and a half Shakti Peethas of Maharashtra. It is reported to be about 60 km from Nashik, so plan it as a day trip.

    Sources: Nashik District site: Saptashrungi; Wikipedia: Saptashrungi

Places to visit in Nashik: frequently asked questions

How many days are enough for Nashik?

Two days covers the main sights: Ramkund and Panchavati, Pandavleni Caves, Sula Vineyards and Trimbakeshwar, with time for food. One day works if you choose between the temples and the wine side, and a third day adds the Western Ghats at Igatpuri or Bhandardara. See our 1, 2 and 3-day itineraries and the two-day itinerary.

What are the top 5 places in Nashik?

Trimbakeshwar Temple, Ramkund and Panchavati, Kalaram Temple, Pandavleni Caves and Sula Vineyards are the places most visitors start with. They cover pilgrimage, heritage and wine; the order is our editors' judgement, not a ranking from visitor numbers.

What are the places near Nashik within 50 km?

Trimbakeshwar is about 29–30 km from the Nashik CBS (verified on Google Maps, 22 Nov 2026). Sula Vineyards, Pandavleni Caves, Someshwar Waterfall, Gangapur Dam, Anjaneri Hill and the Coin Museum are close to the city or on the Trimbakeshwar road, and Igatpuri is reported at about 45 km. We have not measured those, so check a maps app. Bhandardara (about 70–73 km, reported) and Saptashrungi (about 60 km, reported) are further out.

Is Nashik worth visiting?

Yes, for pilgrimage, heritage and wine, and for the hill country around Igatpuri. Nashik hosts the Simhastha Kumbh Mela, with the official Amrut Snan dates in August and September 2027 (see the Kumbh hub). Our longer guide covers the case in detail.

Pandavleni Caves

Pandavleni is a group of caves cut into a hillside south of Nashik city — chambers, halls and carved facades worked directly into the rock. It is the oldest thing you can visit in Nashik, and the least crowded.

Why visit Pandavleni Caves

Pandavleni is the part of Nashik that most visitors skip, which is exactly why it is worth going. The caves are cut into a hill above the plain, and the climb up is rewarded with a long view back over the city.

It is also a complete change of register from the rest of a Nashik trip. After the density of Panchavati and the crowds at Trimbakeshwar, a quiet hillside of empty stone rooms is a genuine relief.

Things to see and do

The caves run along the hillside and are explored on foot, one chamber to the next. Reaching them involves a climb, so footwear matters more here than anywhere else in Nashik.

History and significance

The caves are rock-cut monastic chambers, carved into the hillside long before anything else you can visit in Nashik was built. They are the clearest surviving evidence of how old settlement in this part of Maharashtra is.

How to reach Pandavleni Caves

Pandavleni Caves is in Nashik district, Maharashtra. Nashik itself is reachable by train, bus, road and air, and our how to reach Nashik guide sets out the options from Mumbai, Pune and Delhi.

Best time to visit

Nashik's year divides fairly clearly into the monsoon, the cool months that follow it, and a hot stretch before the rains return. Our month-by-month guide to Nashik covers how that plays out across the district.

Travel tips

  • There is a climb involved. Wear something you can walk up a hill in.
  • There is little shade on the way up. Early morning or late afternoon is far more comfortable than the middle of the day.
  • Carry water. Facilities on the hill are limited.

Frequently asked questions

Where are the Pandavleni Caves?

They are cut into a hillside south of Nashik city, in Nashik district, Maharashtra.

Is there a climb to reach the caves?

Yes — the caves are partway up a hill and are reached on foot. Allow more time and better footwear than a city sight would need.

How do the caves fit into a short Nashik trip?

They work well as a half-day alongside the city. Our two-day itinerary shows one way to sequence it.

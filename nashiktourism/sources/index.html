Sources

Where our information comes from

The official sources behind this site. Use these links to check anything here for yourself — they, not NashikTourism.com, are the source of truth.

Social media

Social-media posts are never treated as confirmation. If a post is the only place something appears, we say so and do not present it as fact.

Our editorial policy explains how these sources are ranked, and what we do when they disagree. Links open the source’s own site in this tab.

Tier 1 · Official

Government bodies, authorities, railways, airports and the venues’ own official sites. Where these disagree with anything else, they win.

  • Airports Authority of India Airport checked weekly Airport information; Nashik (Ozar) is listed in AAI's aeronautical information publication as VAOZ
  • Shri Trimbakeshwar temple trust / Devasthan Official link to be added Attractions checked weekly Darshan timings, special-darshan arrangements, festival closures
  • Government of Maharashtra Government checked weekly State government portal: orders, schemes, announcements
  • Maharashtra Directorate General of Information and Public Relations Government checked daily State press releases and announcements
  • Nashik District Administration Government checked weekly District notices, tourism pages, how to reach Nashik
  • Nashik Divisional Commissioner — Acts and Government Resolutions Government checked daily Government Resolutions and acts concerning the Simhastha
  • Nashik Divisional Commissioner — Administrative Approvals Government checked daily Sanctioned works and amounts for the Simhastha development plan
  • Nashik Municipal Corporation Official link to be added Government checked daily City civic notices, ghat and sanitation arrangements, local traffic notices
  • Press Information Bureau, Government of India Government checked daily Union Government releases, including Ministry of Railways Simhastha preparations
  • Nashik Divisional Commissioner — Contact details (Kumbh Mela Authority) Kumbh Mela checked monthly Where to send questions about Simhastha arrangements
  • Nashik–Trimbakeshwar Kumbh Mela Authority (Office of the Divisional Commissioner, Nashik) Kumbh Mela checked daily Authority constitution, Simhastha arrangements, official announcements
  • All India Radio News (Prasar Bharati) News checked daily Public-broadcaster reporting on Maharashtra and the Simhastha
  • Maharashtra Police Police checked weekly Police notices, advisories
  • Nashik City Police and Nashik Rural Police Official link to be added Police checked daily Traffic diversions, parking plans and crowd-control notices for the Mela
  • Central Railway (Mumbai and Bhusawal divisions serve Nashik) Railways checked daily Zone notices, special trains, cancellations
  • IRCTC — train booking Railways checked by an editor as needed Booking — linked for travellers, not monitored
  • Indian Railways Railways checked weekly Railway notices and special-train announcements
  • National Train Enquiry System Railways checked by an editor as needed Live running status and timetable lookup — linked for travellers, not monitored
  • Maharashtra Public Works Department Roads checked weekly State road works, including the Nashik Ring Road and Trimbak Road widening reviews
  • National Highways Authority of India Roads checked weekly National highway works and advisories
  • 112 India — Emergency Response Support System Safety checked monthly National single emergency number
  • Maharashtra Tourism (MTDC) Tourism checked weekly State tourism information and MTDC properties
  • Maharashtra State Road Transport Corporation (MSRTC) Transport checked daily State bus services and any special Kumbh services
  • Nashik District — How to reach (tourism) Transport checked weekly District tourism guidance on air, rail and road access
  • India Meteorological Department Weather checked by an editor as needed Official forecasts and warnings — linked from /nashik-now/ next to the automated weather

Tier 2 · Established news

Used to find out what is happening. A news report is a lead; it becomes a fact here only when an official source supports it.

  • Free Press Journal — Pune / Nashik coverage News checked daily Established daily; detailed Nashik and Simhastha coverage. News leads become candidates, never published facts.
  • Lokmat Times — Nashik News checked daily Established publication; Nashik coverage. News leads become candidates, never published facts.

Tier 3 · A business’s own information

Hotels, vineyards, restaurants and tours describing themselves. We prefer this to third-party listings.

  • Sula Vineyards (official site) Attractions checked monthly Tour, tasting and visiting information published by the estate

Data providers

Automated feeds used for numbers (such as the weather on Nashik Now), not for official statements.

  • Open-Meteo Weather checked hourly Model-based weather data shown on /nashik-now/. Not an official forecast.

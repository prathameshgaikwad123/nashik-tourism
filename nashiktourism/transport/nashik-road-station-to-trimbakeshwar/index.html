Nashik Road Railway Station to Trimbakeshwar: Distance, Bus, Auto and Cab Options

The last leg from Nashik's main railhead to the Jyotirlinga, compared option by option.

By the NashikTourism.com Editorial Team · Last updated 22 Nov 2026 · Published 22 Nov 2026 · Last verified: Verified 22 Nov 2026

Quick answer

Nashik Road railway station is about 37–39 km from Trimbakeshwar by road, roughly 1 hour 15 minutes on Google Maps (22 November 2026), further than the city centre. The usual choices are a taxi direct, or a bus or auto to the CBS and then an MSRTC bus. We have no verified fare or last-bus time.

How to read the figures on this page

Each figure carries its own status. Verified means an editor read the cited source and it supports the figure. Reported means the named sources publish it but an editor has not yet confirmed it. Routes, fares and timetables change; confirm with the operator before you travel. See how we mark what we know and our sources.

Most visitors reach Nashik by train at Nashik Road, the city’s main railway station, and many want Trimbakeshwar (also written Trimbak, Tryambakeshwar or Trayambakam) the same day. Trimbakeshwar has no direct rail service that we could find, so the last leg is by road. This guide compares the ways to do it. For the other starting points, see the Nashik to Trimbakeshwar distance guide.

How do you reach Trimbakeshwar by bus via the CBS?

The erail station page for Nashik Road describes the public-transport route as a bus from the station to the Nashik CBS (Central Bus Stand) and then a bus to Trimbakeshwar (erail). In practice:

  1. Leave the station and ask for the bus or auto to the CBS. We have not verified the station’s exits, bus bays or any prepaid counter, so check the signs on arrival.
  2. At the CBS, ask the enquiry desk for the Trimbakeshwar bus and platform. MSRTC publishes its own service information (see our sources).
  3. In Trimbakeshwar, confirm the last bus back before you queue for darshan. We do not publish a last-bus time because we have none that is verified.

How long does it take from Nashik Road to Trimbakeshwar?

Google Maps showed 1 hr 15 min to 1 hr 21 min by road from the station on the late morning of 22 November 2026; times vary with traffic. Add a buffer for the change at the CBS if you go by bus. We have no verified bus timetable. On busy days the time can be much longer.

What changes during Kumbh Mela 2027?

Nashik Road is the station most Kumbh travellers are expected to use. The Railways describe special trains as planned (reported; no timetable located), and no official road-traffic, parking or shuttle plan for the Nashik–Trimbakeshwar route has been located. Expect crowds at the station and on the road on the Amrut Snan (Amrut Snan) dates, and check live updates and the Kumbh Mela 2027 hub before you travel.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Nashik Road railway station from Trimbakeshwar?

About 37–39 km by road, roughly 1 hour 15 minutes (Google Maps driving routes of 37.3, 38.0 and 38.8 km, measured 22 November 2026; times vary with traffic).

How do I get from Nashik Road station to Trimbakeshwar?

Take a taxi direct, or go by bus or auto to the Nashik CBS and then an MSRTC bus to Trimbakeshwar. Confirm bus details with MSRTC; we publish no timetable or fares.

Is there a train from Nashik Road to Trimbakeshwar?

We have not found a direct rail service to Trimbakeshwar. Nashik Road is the nearest major station; continue by road.

What is the last bus from Trimbakeshwar back to Nashik?

We have no verified last-bus time. Ask at the Trimbakeshwar bus stand or check with MSRTC on the day, and keep a taxi as a fallback.

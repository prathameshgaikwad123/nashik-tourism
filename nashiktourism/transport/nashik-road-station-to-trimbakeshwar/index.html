Nashik Road Railway Station to Trimbakeshwar: Distance, Bus, Auto and Cab Options

The last leg from Nashik's main railhead to the Jyotirlinga, compared option by option.

By Prathamesh Gaikwad, Editor · Last updated 22 Nov 2026 · Published 22 Nov 2026 · Last verified: Not yet verified against official sources

Quick answer

Nashik Road railway station is about 36–40 km from Trimbakeshwar by road, further than the city centre. The usual choices are a taxi direct, or a bus or auto to the CBS and then an MSRTC bus. We have no verified journey time, fare or last-bus time, so confirm before you travel.

How to read the figures on this page

Each figure carries its own status. Verified means an editor read the cited source and it supports the figure. Reported means the named sources publish it but an editor has not yet confirmed it. Most visitors reach Nashik by train at Nashik Road, the city’s main railway station, and many want Trimbakeshwar (also written Trimbak, Tryambakeshwar or Trayambakam) the same day. Trimbakeshwar has no direct rail service that we could find, so the last leg is by road. This guide compares the ways to do it. For the other starting points, see the Nashik to Trimbakeshwar distance guide.

The reported figures run from about 36 km to about 40 km depending on the source and the route counted. Because Nashik Road is several kilometres from the city centre, it is further than the CBS (about 28–30 km, reported). Trimbakeshwar to Nashik Road railway station is the same distance in the other direction.

How do you reach Trimbakeshwar by bus via the CBS?

The erail station page for Nashik Road describes the public-transport route as a bus from the station to the Nashik CBS (Central Bus Stand) and then a bus to Trimbakeshwar (erail). In practice:

  1. Leave the station and ask for the bus or auto to the CBS. We have not verified the station’s exits, bus bays or any prepaid counter, so check the signs on arrival.
  2. At the CBS, ask the enquiry desk for the Trimbakeshwar bus and platform. MSRTC publishes its own service information (see our sources).
  3. In Trimbakeshwar, confirm the last bus back before you queue for darshan. We do not publish a last-bus time because we have none that is verified.

How long does it take from Nashik Road to Trimbakeshwar?

We have no verified journey time from the station. The city-centre drive is estimated at 45–60 minutes in normal traffic (not yet verified), and Nashik Road adds distance on top of that, so plan on more, and add a buffer for the change at the CBS if you go by bus. On busy days the time can be much longer.

What changes during Kumbh Mela 2027?

Nashik Road is the station most Kumbh travellers are expected to use. The Railways describe special trains as planned (reported; no timetable located), and no official road-traffic, parking or shuttle plan for the Nashik–Trimbakeshwar route has been located. Expect crowds at the station and on the road on the reported Amrit Snan dates, and check live updates and the Kumbh Mela 2027 hub before you travel.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Nashik Road railway station from Trimbakeshwar?

About 36–40 km by road (reported by the Nashik District tourism site, Housing.com and DistancesFrom; not yet measured by our editor).

How do I get from Nashik Road station to Trimbakeshwar?

Take a taxi direct, or go by bus or auto to the Nashik CBS and then an MSRTC bus to Trimbakeshwar. Confirm bus details with MSRTC; we publish no timetable or fares.

Is there a train from Nashik Road to Trimbakeshwar?

We have not found a direct rail service to Trimbakeshwar. Nashik Road is the nearest major station; continue by road.

What is the last bus from Trimbakeshwar back to Nashik?

We have no verified last-bus time. Ask at the Trimbakeshwar bus stand or check with MSRTC on the day, and keep a taxi as a fallback.

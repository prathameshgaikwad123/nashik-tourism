Trimbakeshwar to Panchavati and Ramkund: Distance, Bus, Jeep and Cab Options

Between the two Kumbh Mela sites: distance, options and what to check.

By the NashikTourism.com Editorial Team · Last updated 22 Nov 2026 · Published 22 Nov 2026 · Last verified: Not yet verified against official sources

Quick answer

Trimbakeshwar is about 28–30 km from Panchavati and Ramkund by road, close to the Nashik city figure, because the two are only a short way apart. Go by MSRTC bus via the CBS, shared jeep or cab. We have no verified time, fare or last-bus time; on Kumbh days allow far longer.

How to read the figures on this page

Each figure carries its own status. Verified means an editor read the cited source and it supports the figure. Reported means the named sources publish it but an editor has not yet confirmed it. Dates, from our fact database: Mela opens 31 Oct 2026 · Amrit Snan 2 Aug 2027, 31 Aug 2027, 11 Sep 2027 (Nashik) and 12 Sep 2027 (Trimbakeshwar) · Mela concludes 24 Jul 2028. ◐Reported, not yet confirmed Dates and sources

Panchavati and Ramkund, on the Godavari in Nashik, and Trimbakeshwar (also written Trimbak or Tryambakeshwar), are the two sites of Kumbh Mela 2027, and many pilgrims visit both. This guide covers the journey between them. The full distance table for other starting points is in the Nashik to Trimbakeshwar distance guide.

We have not found a separate published figure for Ramkund or Panchavati. Ramkund is reported to be about 2 km from the Nashik CBS, so the city figure (about 28–30 km) is a reasonable guide. The distance is the same from Ramkund to Trimbakeshwar as from Trimbakeshwar to Ramkund. Tapovan and Kapaleshwar temple are in the same part of the city.

How do you travel between Ramkund and Trimbakeshwar?

  • MSRTC bus: MSRTC buses are reported to run to Trimbakeshwar from the Nashik CBS (erail). Our Ramkund guide and the transport hub note that the Panchavati bus stand is nearer Ramkund; whether Trimbakeshwar buses call there has not been verified. Timings, fares and the last bus back are not verified.
  • Shared jeep: a common way to make the trip; pickup points, frequency and fare are not verified. Ask locally.
  • Cab or taxi: door-to-door from your hotel in Panchavati. Agree the fare, and whether it includes waiting and the return, before you set off.
  • Self-drive: use a maps app for the route; do not assume parking close to Ramkund or the temple on busy days.

How long does the journey take?

The drive from the city is estimated at 45–60 minutes in normal traffic (not yet verified). We have no verified time specifically from Ramkund. Plan on more at peak hours, and much more on festival and Amrit Snan days.

Can you do both Kumbh sites on the same day?

The reported third Amrit Snan has the Vaishnava akharas at Ramkund on 11 September 2027 and the Shaiva akharas at Trimbakeshwar on 12 September 2027 (reported, not confirmed; see the dates table on the Kumbh hub). No official traffic, parking or shuttle plan between the two has been located. On a busy date, consider seeing one site per day rather than both, and check live updates first.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Trimbakeshwar from Ramkund?

About 28–30 km by road, using the Nashik city figure as a guide (reported; no separate Ramkund figure found).

How do I get from Ramkund to Trimbakeshwar?

By MSRTC bus from the CBS, shared jeep or cab. We publish no timetable or fares because none is verified.

How far is Trimbakeshwar from Panchavati?

About the same as from Ramkund, roughly 28–30 km (reported guide figure; not separately confirmed).

Can I visit Ramkund and Trimbakeshwar on the same day during Kumbh?

It is possible on quiet days, but no traffic or shuttle plan has been announced. On busy dates see one site per day and allow far longer.

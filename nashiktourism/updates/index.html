We removed an unsourced Kumbh infrastructure figure

Earlier versions of our Kumbh Mela guide quoted a single government infrastructure figure that we could not trace to an official source. We have removed it. Government development figures for the Simhastha are published at several different scales, so the Kumbh guide now lists each reported figure with what it counts and where it comes from.

Where this comes from

Updates are written from official sources first — the Maharashtra Government, the Nashik district administration, the Kumbh Mela Authority, the railways, the police — and from established news only as a lead. Social media is never treated as confirmation.

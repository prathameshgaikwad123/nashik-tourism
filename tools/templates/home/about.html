What is Nashik known for?

Nashik is a city in Maharashtra, India, built along the banks of the Godavari river. It is known for four quite different things — and most people arrive for one of them, then find themselves staying for the others.

A pilgrimage city on the Godavari

The temples and river ghats of Panchavati draw pilgrims through the year, and Trimbakeshwar — one of the twelve Jyotirlingas — stands in the hills to the west, near the source of the Godavari.

One of the four Kumbh Mela cities

Nashik is one of the four cities that host the Kumbh Mela in rotation. The next Simhastha Kumbh Mela centres on Nashik and Trimbakeshwar, and the city is preparing for it now.

The centre of India's wine country

The hills around the city hold the heart of India's wine industry. Several estates, Sula among the best known, open their vineyards and cellars to visitors for tours and tastings.

A green escape into the Western Ghats

Igatpuri and Bhandardara lie in the hills nearby and turn deep green in the monsoon — which is why Nashik is such a common weekend trip out of Mumbai and Pune.

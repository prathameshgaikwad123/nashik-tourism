The Thread Through It

Everything here follows the river

The Godavari rises in the hills at Trimbakeshwar, beside one of the twelve Jyotirlingas. It runs down onto the plain and through the old quarter of the city at Panchavati, where the ghats at Ramkund step into the water.

That stretch of river is why the Simhastha Kumbh Mela comes to Nashik at all — the bathing follows the Godavari from Kushavarta at Trimbakeshwar down to Ramkund. And the same water, held back in the hills around the city, is what the vineyards and the reservoirs of the Western Ghats are built on.

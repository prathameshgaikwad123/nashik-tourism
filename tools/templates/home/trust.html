About This Site

Why trust this guide

Independent

NashikTourism.com is an independent travel guide. We are not a government body, not an official tourism authority, and not affiliated with any temple trust or state department.

Editorial

Our guides are written to be genuinely useful to travellers and pilgrims. Where we link to a booking site, it is because we think it is the practical option — not because a listing was paid for.

Check before you travel

Timings, fees and festival arrangements change, and Kumbh Mela plans in particular are still being finalised. Please confirm details with official sources close to your travel date.
